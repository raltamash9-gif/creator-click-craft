# PROJECT TITLE

ALTAR STUDIO

Premium Interactive Thumbnail Design Portfolio

(Designed to attract high-paying freelance YouTube creators)

---

# OBJECTIVE

You are an award-winning UI/UX designer, Creative Director, Frontend Engineer, Motion Designer, Branding Expert, and Conversion Rate Optimization specialist.

Your task is to design and develop a world-class interactive portfolio website for a freelance YouTube Thumbnail Designer.

This website is NOT a generic portfolio.

It is a client acquisition website.

Its primary purpose is to convince YouTube creators to hire the designer for premium thumbnail design services.

The website should feel like a combination of

• Apple

• Awwwards

• Framer

• Linear

• Figma

• Netflix

• Behance Featured Projects

while maintaining its own original identity.

Do NOT copy existing websites.

Instead, use them only as inspiration for polish, layout quality and interaction design.

The overall experience should immediately communicate

• Premium quality

• Trust

• Creativity

• Professionalism

• High-end service

• Cinematic storytelling

The visitor should instantly think

"This designer creates thumbnails that make people click."

NOT

"This person knows Photoshop."

Always focus on solving the client's business problem instead of showcasing software skills.

---

# TARGET CLIENTS

The website is designed specifically for

• Small YouTube creators

• Growing YouTube channels

• Documentary creators

• Gaming creators

• History channels

• Technology channels

• Educational channels

• Faceless YouTube channels

Avoid designing for graphic designers.

Design for business owners and creators.

# DESIGN GOAL

Every page should increase the probability that the visitor contacts the designer.
